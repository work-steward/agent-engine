# Community code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of identity, background, experience, or opinion. Give specific, constructive feedback; assume good faith and accept corrections. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in project issues, PRs, discussions, and project-related community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn, restrict, or remove participants proportionately, explaining decisions when privacy permits. Anyone handling a report must protect the reporter's privacy and recuse themselves when personally involved.

Report concerns privately to the project lead using a contact method listed on https://github.com/sohambhattacharjee. If no private contact is available, open a neutral issue requesting a private contact without identifying people or including sensitive details. This temporary contact process must be replaced with a tested private reporting channel before public launch. Appeals may be submitted privately to the project lead; an independent escalation contact remains a public-launch prerequisite.
