# Community code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of identity, background, experience, or opinion. Give specific, constructive feedback; assume good faith and accept corrections. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in project issues, PRs, discussions, and project-related community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn, restrict, or remove participants proportionately, explaining decisions when privacy permits. Report handlers must protect the reporter's privacy and disclose conflicts of interest.

Email reports or appeals privately to [1.bhattacharjee.soham@gmail.com](mailto:1.bhattacharjee.soham@gmail.com). Initially, [@sohambhattacharjee](https://github.com/sohambhattacharjee) handles both; independent project appeals are not yet available. State the concern and requested remedy without publishing private details. An independent appeal contact will be added as the maintainer group grows.
